FoldNet Workspace Manual
📌 Dual synchronized Viewports

Left viewport shows the exact coordinates of the target experimental PDB colored by True Secondary Structure. The right viewport overlays FoldNet's predicted structural annotations on the same geometry.

🔍 Selection and Residue Linking

Hovering or clicking a residue in the sequence or 3D canvas highlights it across all panels (Sequence, both 3D viewers, contact map, and the Residue Inspector).

🎯 Contact Lines Color Code
  • Cyan Lines: Predicted contacts matching ground truth (True Positives).
  • Red Lines: Predicted contacts absent in native structure (False Positives).
  • Orange Lines: Real contacts missed by the model (False Negatives).
